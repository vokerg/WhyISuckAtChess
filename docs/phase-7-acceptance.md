## Supported product and claim boundary

The accepted journey is authenticated connection/import -> durable indexing/timing/engine/evidence -> current immutable diagnosis hierarchy -> owned summary, drill-down, exact-control comparisons and referenced replay. Import completion alone is not diagnosis readiness. Missing, partial, stale, processing, retry and insufficient evidence remain distinct states. Preserve source clocks, exact controls, ratings, timestamps, owned game/ply identities and engine/policy provenance throughout.

The [candidate registry](diagnosis-candidate-projection.md) supports OPEN-002/003, TIME-001–007, RATING-001/002 and SESSION-001–003, with registered synthesis/ranking boundaries. Per-game material, tactical, defensive, conversion, opening and phase/endgame evidence does not automatically imply a recurring cross-game diagnosis. The following remain explicitly unsupported canonical producers: TACT-001–006, OPEN-001/004, CONV-001–003, PHASE-001, END-001–003 and CAL-001 (authoritative user timezone absent). Unsupported is not measured zero incidence. Completing these producers is outside this closeout.

Evidence grades are deterministic sample/coverage grades, not statistical confidence. Exact-control/increment differences and session deterioration are observations, not causal or psychological conclusions. RATING-002 discloses composition rather than statistically adjusting it. Representative examples and priority scores do not independently establish diagnosis truth.
